How this connects to his cameras

  • Vehicle list, status and location come from Device and DeviceStatusInfo.
  • Recent events come from CameraEvent, linked to each exception.
  • "Grab clip" sends a CameraEventCaptureRequest for the last 10 seconds.
  • Continuous live video opens in MyGeotab, since Geotab hasn't published a live-stream API yet.
  • "Feeds paused" stops every stream at once so nobody leaves the wall running overnight.
